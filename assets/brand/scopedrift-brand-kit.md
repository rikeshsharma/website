# scopedrift brand kit

## The idea

**scopedrift is a knowledge and storytelling brand for the moment a perspective shifts.** Its visual language follows a simple movement: curiosity opens the field, stories drift through it, and the centre holds a clear truth.

The logo is called the **Drift Aperture**. The warm circular field represents a scope; the soft radial breaks create movement without becoming a literal gear; its calm negative centre represents the point of insight.

Keep the core mark terracotta and cream. Gold belongs to the surrounding visual language, not the core logo.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Fraunces, 600–700 — thoughtful, literary, and warm.
- **UI and body:** Manrope, 400–700 — contemporary and highly legible.
- **References, dates, and notes:** IBM Plex Mono, sparingly.

Use generous line-height and broad cream space. Prefer sentence case and clear editorial writing over dense product-language.

## Logo rules

- Preserve a clear space equal to the centre-void diameter around the symbol or lockup.
- Keep the full lockup at least 150 px wide digitally. Use the symbol below that.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or recolour the mark with a third core colour.
- The mark should read as a lens or aperture first; avoid pairing it with literal cogs, brains, lightbulbs, or generic “innovation” imagery.

## Supporting visual language

Use thin orbital lines, rounded radial breaks, restrained paper grain, and one small gold “truth point” when a composition needs emphasis. Photography and illustration should feel observant, human, and reflective: marginal notes, landscapes, maps, hands, light, and moments of discovery.

The supplied `scopedrift-key-visual.png` was generated as a supporting editorial image, not as a replacement for the vector logo.
