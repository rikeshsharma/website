Career

  1. 2023 — Present

    Software Engineer

    Samsung Electronics India

    Samsung was where I got my first deep dive into browser engineering. I learned how modern browsers work behind the scenes and had the opportunity to contribute to Samsung Browser on both Android and PC, working across the Extensions, Navigation, Loader, and Downloads modules while building features and tackling complex engineering problems.

    Navigation and Loader

    I contributed to Samsung Browser by working on webpage navigation and loading, with a focus on features such as Prefetching, Preloading, Back/Forward Cache (BFCache), and User-Agent handling to deliver a faster and more reliable browsing experience.

    Downloads

    I contributed to Samsung Browser by working on download management and insecure download protection

    Extension

    I learned how browser extensions interact with the browser internals. I worked on Samsung Browser by developing extension APIs, building features across the native and extension layers, and integrating AI-powered capabilities to enhance the browsing experience.
